# 📊 HR Analytics - Why Employees Leave (Dashboard)

# Project Overview
This Power BI project aims to analyze employee attrition data to identify key factors contributing to employee turnover. By examining various metrics such as job satisfaction, work-life balance, salary slabs, and experience, the dashboard provides actionable insights to help HR teams retain talent and lower attrition rates.

# Key Metrics & Insights
- **Total Employees:** 1,403
- **Attrition Count:** 227
- **Attrition Rate:** 16%
- **Active Employees:** 1,176
- **Average Employee Age:** 37 Years
- **Average Working Experience:** 7 Years

# Major Findings:
1. **OverTime Impact:** Employees working overtime experience significantly higher attrition compared to non-overtime staff.
2. **Work-Life Balance & Job Satisfaction:** Lower satisfaction scores (1 & 2) show a strong correlation with higher employee leave rates.
3. **Salary & Experience Slabs:** Attrition is more prominent among mid-level salary ranges (6-10 LPA) and employees with 0 to 4 years at the company.
